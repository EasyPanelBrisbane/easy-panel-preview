Understanding Composite Decking Costs in Brisbane

Your composite decking cost comes down to how much material your deck needs. Once you know your deck size, you can work out how many boards and how much joist framing to allow for, then get an exact price from Easy Panel with a free quote.

This guide walks through what goes into a composite decking order and how to estimate quantities for a Brisbane deck using the figures from our specification sheet.

What Goes Into a Composite Decking Order?

There are three parts to every deck.

  • The boards. Easy Panel’s boards are 5.4m long, 140mm wide and 20mm thick, in charcoal, mocha or light grey, with an embossed timber grain or sanded brushed finish.
  • The fixings. Boards are fixed with stainless steel hidden fasteners that set a 4mm gap between boards.
  • The frame. The specification sheet calls for joists at 350mm to 400mm centres, with at least 50mm of ground clearance.

Easy Panel keeps all decking components in stock, so once your order is confirmed it can be delivered to your door within 24 to 48 hours.

How Do You Work Out How Much Composite Decking You Need?

You can estimate your order with a tape measure and a few simple numbers. Each board covers 144mm across the deck (a 140mm board plus the 4mm gap). From there:

Item Per square metre of deck Working
Lineal metres of board About 6.9m 1m ÷ 0.144m
Number of 5.4m boards About 1.3 boards 6.9m ÷ 5.4m
Joists at 400mm centres About 2.5 lineal metres 1m ÷ 0.4m
Joists at 350mm centres About 2.9 lineal metres 1m ÷ 0.35m

Worked Example: a 5.4m x 4.8m Deck

Say you are building a deck 5.4m long and 4.8m wide, about 26 square metres, with the boards running 5.4m length.

  • Boards: 4.8m across divided by 144mm is 33.3, so you need 34 full-length boards. Because the deck matches the 5.4m board length, there are no butt joins.
  • Joists: at 400mm centres along the 5.4m length, you need 15 joists, each 4.8m long. That is 72 lineal metres of joist.

How Can You Keep Your Composite Decking Cost Down?

Two things make a real difference to the cost.

Plan Around the Board Length

Boards are 5.4m long. If your deck is 5.4m or shorter in the direction the boards run, every board can go down full length with no butt joins, as in the worked example above.

Install It Yourself

At Easy Panel, our decking is designed for easy DIY installation. The concealed clip system speeds up installation and keeps fixings hidden, so there are no visible screws or nails across the finished deck.

What Does Composite Decking Cost to Maintain?
