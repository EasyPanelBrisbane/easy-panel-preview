Composite vs Timber Decking: Cost, Upkeep and Appearance

Choose composite decking if avoiding regular oiling and staining is a priority. Choose timber if you prefer natural grain and want the option to refinish it. The better buy also depends on which timber or composite board you’re comparing, what the deck is exposed to and what’s included in the quote.

Composite and timber decking at a glance

Upfront cost
Timber prices vary with species, grade and availability. With composite, price the boards together with the specified clips, screws and finish boards. Compare the total for the same deck layout.
Ongoing care
Composite boards generally need cleaning rather than periodic oiling. Timber care depends on the species and finish. If you want timber to retain its original colour, budget for the recommended coating and renewal schedule.
Appearance and repair
Timber has natural variation between boards and can often be sanded and refinished, depending on the board and its condition. Composite typically has a more consistent finish. Ask how damaged boards are replaced and whether you can order a spare from the same batch.
Sun and wet areas
Both materials can get hot in full sun. Compare samples where the deck will sit. For a poolside deck or an exposed stair, check product-specific slip information for the intended use and ask about drainage.

How to compare decking quotes

Give each supplier the same dimensions, board direction and edge details. Include any stairs or fascia and say whether the existing frame is staying. Ask for an itemised quote so you can see which of these costs are included.

  • Deck boards, cutting waste, edge boards and trims.
  • Clips or screws, delivery and installation labour.
  • Any changes to joists or framing needed for your chosen board and fixing system.
  • Initial finishing and the cost of future care over the same number of years.

A cheaper board can still produce a higher quote if the layout creates more offcuts or requires changes to the frame. For more detail, see our Brisbane composite decking cost guide.

Which should you choose?

For a busy outdoor dining area, compare stain care and cleaning instructions. For an exposed deck, ask how each finish weathers and inspect samples in direct sun. If the project uses an existing timber frame, check its condition and compatibility before ordering either type of board.
