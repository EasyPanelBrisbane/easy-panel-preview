The Modern Building Material

Our roofing panels combine ceiling, insulation and roof into a single modern product. The panels are constructed from an insulating material, sandwiched between steel roofing and ceiling layers. Each panel fits and locks neatly together to form an attractive, waterproof roof.

Strong yet lightweight, the panels can span large distances of four meters and more. And with fewer structural supports needed, you’ll create an open, uncluttered commercial space with greater design potential.

Easy Panel roofing panels are also quick and simple to install. Your project kit conveniently includes fixings, flashings, screws, rivets, washers, seals and trim, making it easy to finish your project professionally.

Functional and Versatile Panels

Insulated roofing panels bring significant advantages to commercial projects. With the ability to span large distances, the panels are an ideal roofing solution for large sheds and workshops, or cool outdoor areas for staff to enjoy their lunch breaks.

The high-quality finish and large span length also make them ideal for customers to browse or dine beneath. In any building project, Easy Panel insulated roofing panels are a cost-effective investment designed to look good for years to come.

Better Insulation by Design

With rising energy costs and increasing regulation on energy efficiency, it makes sense to switch to smarter, modern solutions. All-in-one insulated roofing panels are simply a better building material, with a diversity of uses.

With an innovative design, Easy Panel products are efficiently manufactured to provide a cost-effective, high-quality product. Once installed, the high thermal efficiency of the panels helps regulate the ambient temperature and reduce energy bills.
