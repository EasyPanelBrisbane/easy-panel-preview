Flashings and cappings

C Channel — 1.0 mm

Roll-formed from 4 m powder-coated steel in a Surfmist-type colour. Provides a connection between the existing structure and the Easy Panel end.

C Channel — 0.5 mm

Roll-formed from 4 m powder-coated steel in a Surfmist-type colour. Provides a connection between the existing structure and the Easy Panel end.

Barge Flashing

Roll-formed from 4 m, 0.5 mm powder-coated steel in a Surfmist-type colour. Provides a finished, weather-resistant edge to the sides of Easy Panel.

Z Flashing

Roll-formed from 4 m, 0.5 mm powder-coated steel in a Surfmist-type colour. Provides a drip-resistant capping at the lower end of Easy Panel.
